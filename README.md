## ✦ 𝗔𝗕𝗢𝗨𝗧 𝗠𝗘

> **Engineering Elegance through Simplicity & Performance.**

I am a Software Engineer and Open Source Contributor who believes in building robust, highly optimized systems. I specialize in crafting modern, professional UI designs with a deep appreciation for foundational web technologies. 

- 🔭 **Currently building:** **Invisible Concierge** (Room Management System) & a comprehensive **School ERP**.
- 💡 **Philosophy:** Emphasizing pure HTML/CSS implementations for ultimate control and performance, avoiding heavy frameworks when unnecessary.
- ⚙️ **Workflow:** Heavily leveraging **Spec-Driven Development** for clean architecture and predictable scaling.
- 🌱 **Continuously learning:** Advanced AI integrations, system refactoring, and managing technical debt.
